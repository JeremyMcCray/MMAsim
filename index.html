ON THE GROUND

Takedown key + a direction starts a pass, sweep, escape or stand-up; for a short window the other fighter can deny it with the same direction + the takedown key (the compass on screen shows which): one key, your move when you are free, your read while theirs is up.Landing moves, denying them and landing strikes build momentum and advantage, which shrink the other fighter's windows. Takedown key alone goes for a submission (deny it with S + the takedown key). Once it is locked: hold the takedown key to squeeze and tap a direction to crank; the defender holds block to fight the hands and answers a crank with its direction + the takedown key. Block covers up and never reads; reading leaves you open. Hands punch; hold Q for elbows, E for hammer fists, R for body shots; leg keys knee the body. Strikes hit hardest from mount; the bottom fighter can only strike from guard. The referee stands up a stalled fight after eight seconds.
