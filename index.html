OPTIONS
Press ESC at any time to open or close this panel. In practice the fight pauses.

KEY BINDINGS

Click a key to rebind it. Backspace clears a slot, Esc cancels.

STRIKES

Each limb key throws a different strike depending on which modifier you are holding. Strikes only do damage when the hand or foot actually reaches the target: step in to add power, and don't throw long shots from the clinch. Press the next strike while one is still landing to chain it.

ON THE GROUND

Positions: closed guard → half guard → side control → mount, or back control. Takedown key + a direction attempts a pass, escape, sweep or stand-up (the other fighter holds block to base and deny it; attempts have a short cooldown). Takedown key alone goes for a submission from that position; hold it to squeeze, the defender holds block and times escapes. The hands punch; hold Q for elbows, E for hammer fists, R for body shots; leg keys knee the body. Strikes hit hardest from mount; the bottom fighter can only strike from guard.
